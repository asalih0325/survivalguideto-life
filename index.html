About the host

Ammaar Salih

Ammaar Salih started Survival Guide to Life in May 2020 around one question: how do you win in life every second, physically, mentally, spiritually, financially and consistently?

Each episode is a long-form conversation with someone who has worked out part of that answer. Life coaches, doctors, financial pros, founders, artists and survivors sit down with Ammaar to talk through what actually moved the needle for them, and what you can take from it.

The show covers all five pillars on purpose. A strong body with no money plan, or a big bank account with no peace of mind, isn't winning. The goal is to keep all five moving at once, every day.
